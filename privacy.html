Privacy policy

Household Agent is a private app. Its only users are the members of the household that runs it, and it only accesses the Google account they own.

  • Data read from Google APIs is used only to carry out requests made by the household members.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Credentials and data stay in the owners own Google account and on their private home systems.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
